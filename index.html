NienCoin

A peer-to-peer digital currency

NienCoin is an independent Proof-of-Work Layer-1 blockchain designed for decentralized digital value transfer.

No central bank. No single server. A blockchain maintained by its participants.

Read the Whitepaper Download

What is NienCoin?

NienCoin is an independent digital currency operating on its own blockchain.

Transactions are recorded on a public Proof-of-Work blockchain.

Learn more →

How does it work?

Miners use computational work to produce blocks. Nodes verify those blocks and maintain the blockchain.

Participants communicate through a peer-to-peer network.

Learn about mining →

Why NienCoin?

NienCoin is designed around simplicity, limited supply, open verification and Proof-of-Work security.

Anyone with compatible software can participate in the network.

Design goals →
NienCoin Network

The NienCoin network consists of independent nodes, miners and users communicating through a peer-to-peer network.

Limited Supply

The maximum supply of NienCoin is 20,000,000 NEC.

New coins are introduced through valid Proof-of-Work block rewards.

Mining

Miners compete to produce valid blocks and receive block rewards according to the consensus rules.

Mining also contributes computational security to the network.

Smallest Unit

NienCoin is divisible into very small units.
